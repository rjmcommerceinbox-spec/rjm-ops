# RJM Commerce – Weekly Operating Instruction
Source: https://claude.ai/artifact/GXNL6Y3LCNdLGrLQbZHc6E (copy as of 2026-11-26)

One weekly run, every **Monday at 08:00**. Rob opens the MacBook and logs in first, so the SP-API connection and the Mac folders are available. Claude gathers everything **once**, then reports. **Nothing is sent, ordered, listed, repriced or paid without Rob's approval.**

## 1. Rules to save usage
- Pull each data source **once** per run. Don't repeat a search or report already done that week.
- Read the latest summary notes first (decision log, last week's dashboard), not full history.
- Between Monday runs: short answers only. No new research unless Rob asks.
- Use Supermetrics or the SP-API for figures, not web searches. Perplexity, Firecrawl or web search only for questions the data can't answer.
- Keep outputs short: one dashboard, one action list. Long documents only when Rob asks.
- Write one weekly note (`weekly/weekly_YYYY-MM-DD.md` and project `claude/weekly_YYYY-MM-DD.md`) and update the decision log once, at the end.

## 2. Connected apps and what each is for
- **Amazon SP-API / Seller Central (Mac logged in):** sales, stock, fees, buy box, inbound shipments, settlements.
- **Supermetrics:** Amazon sales and advertising figures, if connected.
- **Gmail:** Sam (ExaClair), Natalie (accounts), Pipi, Jing, Sprint – new emails, invoices, quotes. Drafts only.
- **Google Drive / Sheets and Mac folders:** master inventory, accounts workbook, Sam's catalogue and clearance lists.
- **Google Calendar and Reminders:** deadlines (ExaClair order cut-offs, VAT, trade mark).
- **Perplexity / Firecrawl / web search:** market and competitor checks, supplier research, Amazon rule changes.
- **Reply.io:** supplier and wholesale outreach – drafts only.
- **Linear:** action tracking, if Rob wants tasks logged there.
- **Memory, project notes and this repo:** decisions and context, so nothing is asked twice.

## 3. The Monday run – packages
### A. Sales and stock
- Last 7 days: units, sales, profit per SKU; best and worst sellers.
- Stock: days of cover per SKU; flag under 28 days (reorder) and unsold past 45, 60, 90 days (slow-stock ladder).
- Buy box: who holds it and at what price; flag lines where Amazon has sold out.
- Inbound shipments: status and any problems.
### B. Sourcing – Sam's catalogue
- Check Gmail and folders for any new ExaClair list (clearance, price list, new launches).
- Screen only **new or changed** lines against the buy rule: ROI 50%+, profit £2.50+, our sales 2+ a month, Amazon buy box 70% or less.
- Shortlist up to 10 lines with quantity (77 days' cover, rounded to MOQ) and cost.
### C. Listings
- Listings to create for incoming stock (singles, 3-packs, bundles B17–B19).
- Blocked, gated or suppressed listings and what to do.
- Price changes proposed, with reasons – never below the minimum price.
### D. Finance
- Monzo balance, Amazon payouts received and due.
- ExaClair: balance, credit limit and headroom, invoices outstanding.
- Director's loan balance.
- VAT position for the quarter; next deadlines.
- Cash check: can next week's proposed order be paid without new capital?
### E. Dashboard and actions
- **Dashboard (one page):** sales, profit, cash, stock value, ExaClair balance, top 5 and bottom 5 SKUs, versus monthly target.
- **Action list:** numbered, each with owner, cost and deadline, marked **Approve / Decline**. Draft emails ready, not sent.
### F. Weekly protection checks
- **Lost and damaged stock:** units sent vs received; lost, damaged and return records. List every unit Amazon owes for, with a draft claim. Done in-house.
- **Account health:** IPI, order defect rate, policy warnings, listing removals, FBA capacity limits. Flag changes since last week.
- **Returns and reviews:** returns by SKU with reasons; any new review of 3 stars or less. Flag fixes.
- **Amazon selling out of ExaClair lines:** record stock left and price; flag sell-outs with a proposed price. Don't follow Amazon's price down.

## 4. Approval
- Rob replies with the numbers he approves. Claude carries out only those actions.
- Anything that spends money, emails a supplier, changes a live listing or changes the accounts needs a clear yes.

## 5. Fortnightly and monthly extras
- **Fortnightly:** competitor and keyword check (Helium 10, Keepa) on the top 10 lines.
- **Monthly (first Monday):** P&L, cash plan versus target, short review of suppliers and the prep-centre plan.
